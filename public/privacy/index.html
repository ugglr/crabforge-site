Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Crabforge is an internal tool that publishes videos and posts to channels owned by its operator and reads those channels' statistics. This policy explains what data Crabforge accesses when a channel is connected, what it does with that data, and how to remove it.

YouTube API Services

Crabforge uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to Crabforge you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

When a Google account is connected, Crabforge asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the connected channel and manage them (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy and release times, and deletion of videos it uploaded);
  • read the channel's details, videos and playlists;
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, watch time, subscribers and audience retention.

Crabforge also reads public YouTube data, such as public videos and channels, to compare our channels with others.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish our own content to our own channels and to show us how those channels perform. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. No person outside the operator reads it.

Crabforge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we store

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • The channel's id and name.
  • The posts we schedule and the ids of the videos we upload.
  • Daily channel statistics and YouTube Analytics reports, so we can see trends over time.

We do not store the personal data of viewers.

How long we keep it

Tokens are kept while a channel is connected and deleted when it is disconnected. Statistics are kept while the channel is managed by Crabforge and deleted, together with the channel's other data, when it is removed. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days unless the channel's owner has agreed to keep it longer.

How to revoke access

You can revoke Crabforge's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. To have stored data deleted, email us at the address below.

Changes

We update this page when what Crabforge accesses or stores changes, and change the date above when we do.
