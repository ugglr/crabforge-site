crabforge

Publishing and analytics for our own channels

Crabforge is an internal tool. We use it to schedule and publish videos and posts to the social media channels of our own brands, and to read how those channels are doing.

It is not a public service. You cannot sign up, and it only connects to accounts we own.

What it does

For each channel we connect, Crabforge uploads the videos we have made, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and release times, and reads the channel's statistics and analytics so we can see what grows.
